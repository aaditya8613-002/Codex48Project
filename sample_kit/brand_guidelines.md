# Voice and tone

Our voice is calm, clear and practical. We write like a helpful colleague, not like an advertisement. Use short sentences and plain words. Prefer active voice.

Do not use exclamation marks in headlines or in more than one sentence of a post. Never write in all capital letters for emphasis.

# Messaging

Lead with what the reader can do, not with what we built. Describe one benefit per sentence and back it with something specific. Do not compare FlowDesk with named competitors.

Do not use fear-based messaging such as "you will fall behind if you do not switch". Explain the value instead.

# Social media

On LinkedIn keep posts under 700 characters, start with the point, and end with one clear next step. Use at most two hashtags. On X (Twitter) use no more than one hashtag and no emoji in the first line.

# Inclusive language

Write in a way that includes everyone. Avoid idioms that rely on culture or ability, such as "blind spot" or "falling on deaf ears". Describe images in alt text in one short sentence.

# Customer stories

Only publish a customer story after the customer has approved the final text in writing. Quote customers exactly and name their role and company.
